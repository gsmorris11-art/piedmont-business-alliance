For homeowners

How it works

There is no charge to homeowners for asking.

  1. Tell us the job

    Pick the trade, describe the work and say which town you are in.

  2. Get connected

    We pass your request to a member business that does that work in your area.

  3. Hire directly

    The business contacts you, quotes the job and works for you. You deal with a neighbor, not a call center.

For service businesses

More of the right calls, from people nearby

The Alliance is built for owner-operated trades. Members share the cost and the work of bringing in customers, and send each other the jobs they don't do.

Homeowner leads

Requests that come through the Alliance go to members in the matching trade and service area.

Trade-to-trade referrals

The roofer who spots rotted fascia knows a gutter member. The HVAC tech who finds bad wiring knows an electrician.

Face-to-face meetings

Members meet in person, so a referral comes from someone who has shaken your hand.

Shared marketing

One name, one website and one message promoting local trades across the region.
